Privacy Policy for Stork Stats

Effective Date: January 2, 2026

Stork Stats ("we," "us," or "our") operates the mobile application Stork Stats (the "App"). The App is designed to help nurses and healthcare professionals track anonymized delivery statistics, such as counts of deliveries, types of procedures, and shift summaries, for internal hospital or department use. The App does not collect, store, or process any personal information or protected health information (PHI) about patients, nurses, or any individuals.

This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information collection, use, and disclosure when you use the App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects no personal information from users. Specifically:

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account logins are required or collected.
  • No patient identifiers, medical records, or any PHI (as defined under HIPAA) are collected, stored, or transmitted.
  • Delivery tracking data entered into the App is limited to aggregated, anonymized statistics (e.g., number of deliveries per shift, procedure types) and is stored locally on your device only.

We may automatically collect certain non-personal device information when you use the App, such as:

  • Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifier (for troubleshooting and performance improvement).
  • Usage data, such as crash reports and anonymous analytics (e.g., how often features are used), to help us improve the App.

This device information is anonymized and cannot be used to identify you personally.

2. How We Use Collected Information

Any non-personal information collected is used solely for:

  • Improving App functionality and performance.
  • Diagnosing technical issues.
  • Generating anonymous usage statistics for internal development purposes.

We do not use any information for advertising, marketing, or tracking purposes.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, or disclose any information collected by the App with third parties, except:

  • With trusted service providers (e.g., analytics tools) who are contractually bound to use the information only for providing services to us and to keep it confidential. These providers receive only anonymized data.
  • If required by law, such as in response to a subpoena or government request.

Since no personal or health information is collected, there is no risk of disclosing sensitive data.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any non-personal information stored on your device, including encryption where applicable. However, no app or internet transmission is completely secure. Data entered remains on your device unless you choose to export anonymized reports.

5. Data Retention

Anonymized usage data is retained only as long as necessary for App improvement (typically in aggregated form). You can delete all local data by uninstalling the App.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for professional use by healthcare workers and is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 in some jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. International Users

The App is intended for use in regions where it is offered. If you access the App from outside these regions, you do so at your own risk and are responsible for compliance with local laws.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in the App and on our website (if applicable). Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
